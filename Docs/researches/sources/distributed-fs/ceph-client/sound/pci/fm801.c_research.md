# sources/distributed-fs/ceph-client/sound/pci/fm801.c

## Purpose

This file is the ALSA PCI driver for ForteMedia FM801-based sound cards, including Gallant Odyssey Sound 4 IDs. It exposes PCM playback/capture, AC97 mixer and optional secondary codec support, MPU-401 MIDI, OPL3 FM, optional multichannel playback, suspend/resume state, and optional TEA575x radio support including tuner-only boards.

## Important APIs, Types, and Functions

`struct fm801` stores I/O base, IRQ, multichannel and secondary-codec state, tuner flags, playback and capture ping-pong DMA state, AC97/card/PCM/rawmidi pointers, current substream pointers, spinlock, optional V4L2/TEA575x state, and saved PM registers. AC97 helpers are `fm801_ac97_is_ready()`, `fm801_ac97_is_valid()`, `snd_fm801_codec_write()`, and `snd_fm801_codec_read()`. PCM callbacks are the playback/capture prepare, trigger, pointer, open, and close functions, with `snd_fm801_interrupt()` owning ping-pong buffer advancement. Mixer controls use `FM801_SINGLE`, `FM801_DOUBLE`, and `FM801_DOUBLE_TLV` plus a digital capture source enum.

## Control Flow

Probe enables PCI resources, initializes state, detects FM801-AU multichannel capability from revision `>= 0xb1`, resets the primary codec unless tuner-only mode is requested, probes secondary AC97 codec addresses for multichannel cards, requests IRQ and bus mastering for normal audio cards, initializes registers, and configures optional TEA575x support. Tuner-only mode skips PCM, mixer, MIDI, and OPL3 creation. PCM prepare programs control bits, sample-rate index, format bits, channel mode, period count, initial DMA base addresses for buffer 1 and buffer 2, and cached positions. IRQ handling acknowledges status, advances the completed buffer index and logical position, writes the next period address into the freed hardware buffer, and reports period elapsed.

## State and Persistence Behavior

Playback and capture each maintain a two-buffer queue in cached `ply_*` and `cap_*` fields. ALSA-managed DMA buffers are allocated per PCM device. Selected FM801 registers are saved on suspend into `saved_regs[]` and replayed on resume. AC97 state is suspended/resumed through ALSA AC97 helpers unless tuner-only. TEA575x frequency is restored on resume when enabled.

## Dependencies and Integration Points

The driver depends on ALSA core, PCM, AC97, TLV, MPU-401, OPL3, PCI resource management, DMA buffer helpers, low-level I/O ports, optional V4L2/TEA575x APIs, and Linux PM. It adds playback channel maps through `snd_pcm_add_chmap_ctls()`.

## Risks and Test Signals

Risks include tuner-only fallback when AC97 reset fails, secondary codec probing side effects, two-buffer wrap/address off-by-one errors, unimplemented `FM801_IRQ_VOLUME`, and multichannel-only controls depending on revision detection. Test audio and tuner-only probe, AC97 reset and secondary codec detection, PCM playback/capture with varied period sizes, pause/resume, multichannel constraints and chmaps, MPU/OPL3, TEA575x autodetection, suspend/resume, and IRQ wraparound.
