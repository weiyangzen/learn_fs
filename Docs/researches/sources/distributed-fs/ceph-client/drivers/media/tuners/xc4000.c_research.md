# sources/distributed-fs/ceph-client/drivers/media/tuners/xc4000.c

Purpose: firmware-driven Xceive XC4000/XC4100 tuner for analog TV, FM radio, ATSC/QAM, and DVB-T/T2-style tuning.

APIs/functions: exports `xc4000_attach()`. Ops cover init, sleep, digital/analog tune, release, frequency, bandwidth, status, and RF strength. Firmware helpers upload/parse firmware, seek/load base/std/scode images, and verify device state. Register helpers write/read Xceive registers and tune channels.

Control flow/state: attach creates/reuses shared state, applies config, checks product ID, loads firmware for new instances, and installs ops. Digital tuning maps delivery system/bandwidth to RF mode, offset, video standard, and firmware type, then programs source, standard, amplitude/CVBS, and RF. Analog tuning maps V4L2/audio module options to one of 24 standard entries, loads firmware/SCODE, and tunes. Sleep may power down and invalidate current firmware.

Dependencies/integration: firmware loader, I2C, V4L2/DVB, `tuner-i2c.h`, `xc4000.h`, `xc2028-types.h`, and reset callback.

Risks/tests: firmware fallback/corruption, ignored I2C writes during upload, audio-standard module parameters, and shared lifetime. Test product IDs, firmware files, digital bandwidths, analog standards/FM, sleep PM, signal scaling, and reset failures.
