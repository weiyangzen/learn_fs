Ghostscript makefile fragment re-adding contributed Plan 9 drivers. It defines build rules for `plan9.dev` from `gdevplan9.c` and several HP DeskJet 8xx/16xx devices from `gdevcd8.c`.

The comments indicate these drivers are not in the upstream distribution and are reinserted after imports.
